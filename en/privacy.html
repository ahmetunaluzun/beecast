Privacy policy

Version 1.9.0 · Effective 28 October 2026 · Covers every version of the Bee Cast app and this site

In short: Bee Cast collects no data from you and sends nothing to us.

What you enter stays on your device. The app only connects to the IPTV provider you add, to services you turn on (such as Google Drive sync) and, in the Android version installed from this site, to the version file on this site to check for updates.

What we don't collect

  • No account: you don't sign up, and no email address or phone number is asked for.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking code or crash reporting.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • What you watch, what you search for and which provider you use never reach us. We have no server that collects them.

What is stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its own storage on your device:

  • The playlist details you add: playlist URL, server address, username, password, MAC address and programme guide URL.
  • Channel, movie, series and programme lists downloaded from your provider, so they don't have to be downloaded on every launch. When you filter movies by genre, the movie genres asked from your provider are stored too. When a movie's details arrive from your provider (you open its details or its plot is shown), its original title, cast and director are stored as well, so search can find the movie by them. No extra request is made for this; it is not included in backups and is deleted when the playlist is removed.
  • Your favourites, watch history and where you stopped in a movie or episode. The history also keeps how many times and at what times of day you opened something; the "You usually watch at this time", "Watched often" and "Based on what you watched" suggestions are worked out from it on this device and are never sent anywhere. Movies and series you mark as "Watched" are also kept (the "Watched" list and the card badge come from this). If you create profiles, these are kept separately for each profile.
  • The name and colour of the profiles you create, whether each is a kids profile and the kids profile's age band (0–6, 7–12, 13–15), and which profile was last chosen on this device.
  • The name and stream address of radios you add in Settings › Radio.
  • If you turn on phone sync: on the TV, the paired phone's local network address, name and sync key; on the phone, the paired TVs' names, sync keys and last sync time. A record of favourites you remove is also kept for 90 days (so a removed favourite does not come back from the other device when syncing).
  • If you turned on Google Drive sync: the refresh token Google gave this device, the email address of the connected Google account, the ID of the sync file in Drive and the last sync time.
  • Your settings: language, look, text size, audio and subtitle preferences, parental lock PIN.
  • A device code generated on the device for MAC portals.

None of this is sent to us. It only leaves your device if you back it up yourself (see Backup and moving below). Deleting a playlist in Settings also deletes its channels, movies, series, favourites and watch history. Deleting a profile deletes that profile's favourites and watch history. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Backup and moving

Settings › My devices only runs when you start it. A backup contains your playlist details (including passwords), profiles, favourites, watch history, settings and recent searches. The profile chosen on this device is not included. The MAC device code is included only if you choose. Channel and movie lists are not included.

  • Backup file: encrypted with a password you choose. You decide where to send the file (email, Drive and so on); it never reaches us. Without the password the file can't be opened; if you forget it, we can't open it either.
  • Moving between phone and TV (or another phone/tablet): when you tap "Open connection" on the phone, it accepts connections only from devices on the same local network (your home Wi-Fi) and closes the connection when you leave that screen (if phone sync is on, see below). The TV (or the other device, with "Load from another device") scans its own local network to find the phone. Nothing is transferred until you confirm on the phone the code shown on both screens, and the data sent is encrypted. Nothing goes to the internet or to us.
  • Phone sync (only if you turn it on): on the TV, Settings › My devices › Sync with phone pairs once, after you confirm on the phone the code shown on both screens. Then, while the app is open (at start-up, when watching or favourites change, and every 5 minutes), the TV sends the following to the phone on the same local network and takes the phone's: profile names, colours, kids setting and age band; each profile's resume points and favourites; the names and stream addresses of the radios you added in Settings › Radio (a radio you add or delete is added or deleted on your other devices too); your personal settings: app language, audio and subtitle language, whether subtitles are on and their size, the playback buffer and browser identity (User-Agent) setting, the guide time shift and the parental lock PIN. Screen and device settings such as theme, text size, picture-in-picture, start screen and MAC code are not sent and stay separate on each device. If a profile's name or a setting differs between two devices, the most recent change wins. Titles, playlist address, username and password are not sent: content is matched by your provider's content number and the account by a one-way hash of the address and username (for M3U, a hash of the stream address). The data sent is encrypted with the key given at pairing. On a phone with synced TVs the connection stays open while the app is in the foreground and closes when it goes to the background; a new TV can pair only while the backup screen is open. Turn sync off with "Turn off sync" on the TV or "Remove sync" on the phone; your records are not deleted. Nothing goes to the internet or to us. Pairing keys are not included in backups.
  • Google Drive sync (only if you turn it on): when you choose Settings › My devices › Connect Google Drive, a code and the address google.com/device appear on screen; you approve the code in your own Google account. The app asks only for the "drive.file" permission: in your Drive it sees only the file it created, not your other files. A single sync file (beecast-sync.json) is created in a "Bee Cast" folder in your Drive; if you put a playlist backup there, a second file (below). While the app is open (at start-up, when what you watch or your favourites change, and every 15 minutes) the same information as in phone sync (except the parental lock PIN) is written to and read from this file: profile names, colours, kids setting and age band; each profile's resume points and favourites; the names and stream addresses of the radios you added in Settings › Radio (a radio you add or delete is added or deleted on your other devices too); your personal settings: app language, audio and subtitle language, whether subtitles are on and their size, the playback buffer and browser identity (User-Agent) setting, the guide time shift. Screen and device settings such as theme, text size, picture-in-picture, start screen and MAC code are not sent and stay separate on each device. If a profile's name or a setting differs between two devices, the most recent change wins. Content names, playlist address, username and password are not written (content number and account hash, as above). The app does not encrypt the file further, so the parental lock PIN is not written to it (it travels with the encrypted phone sync on your local network and with the encrypted Drive backup). The connection is directly between your device and Google; nothing comes to us. "Disconnect" deletes the token on this device, "Delete sync data" deletes the sync file and the playlist backup in Drive; you can remove Bee Cast's access at any time in your Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com). The connection token is not included in backups.
  • Playlist backup on Drive (only if you put one there): sync does not carry playlist details. When you choose Settings › My devices › Back up to Drive, the same backup as above (your playlist details, including passwords) is encrypted with a backup password you choose and written as a second file (beecast-yedek.json) in the "Bee Cast" folder in your Drive. Only the name of the device that made the backup and its date are readable in the file. The backup password never goes to Drive or to us; without it the file can't be opened, and if you forget it, we can't open it either. When you connect Drive on a new device, the app finds this file; once you type the password, the playlists are added to that device. A new backup replaces the previous one. "Delete sync data" deletes this file too.
  • Backups received from TVs are kept in the app's storage on your phone (at most three per device). You can delete them in Settings › My devices; uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • On Android 17 and later the phone asks for local network permission for this connection. Without it you can still back up with a file.

What the app connects to

Bee Cast only connects to addresses you enter: your playlist or provider server, the programme guide URL, radio addresses you add in Settings › Radio and the streams you play. The app ships no radio list or radio directory of its own. If you use local transfer in My devices or phone sync, it also connects your phone and TV (or your other phone/tablet) over your home's local network. Like any internet connection, these servers see your device's IP address and the app's user agent. Your provider handles that data under its own terms; check its privacy policy.

If you turn on Google Drive sync, the app connects to Google's sign-in (oauth2.googleapis.com) and Drive (www.googleapis.com) servers. Google processes that connection and the file in your Drive under its own privacy policy.

The maker of your TV or phone (for example Samsung or Google), Windows on a PC together with Microsoft Edge WebView2 (Microsoft), which draws the app's screen, and the app store may collect their own data about device and store use under their own policies. That is outside our control.

Casting (Android phone and tablet): when you tap the cast button in the player, the app uses the Google Cast feature of Google Play services to look for Chromecast and Google TV devices on the same local network. If you pick a device, the stream address (depending on your provider, it may contain your username and password), the title and where you stopped in a movie are sent to that device; the device then opens the stream from your provider itself. Discovery and the connection are handled by Google Play services, and Google processes this under its own privacy policy. If you don't use casting, none of this happens; nothing reaches us.

Radio in the car (Android Auto, Android phone): the name, logo address and stream address of your radios (those you add in Settings › Radio and your playlist's radio channels; filtered in a kids profile) are copied into the app's storage on the phone so the car can list them even when the app is closed. While the phone is connected to a car they are listed on the car's screen; the phone opens the radio you pick from your provider itself. The name and playback state shown on the car's screen pass through Android Auto and the car; Google and the car maker handle that under their own policies. If you don't use Android Auto, the list just stays on your phone; nothing reaches us.

If you get the Android or Windows version from the Download page on this site, the file is downloaded from GitHub (GitHub, Inc.); GitHub processes data about that download (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy. We receive no information from that download.

Update check (only the Android version installed from this site): at most once a day, and when you tap "Check for updates" in Settings › About, the app reads the version file on this site (surum.json). The app sends no information in this request; like any connection, GitHub Pages sees your device's IP address. If you choose to install a new version, the file is downloaded from GitHub and you confirm the installation in Android's own window. Only the day of the last check is stored on your device; the downloaded file stays in the app's temporary folder and is deleted at the next update. Versions installed from a store (Samsung, LG, Google Play) are updated by the store and do not run this check.

This site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security when serving the site (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Children

Bee Cast collects no personal data, so it collects none from children either. You can hide adult content with the parental lock; it applies to content your provider marks as adult. A kids profile also hides categories whose name clearly says XXX, 18+, Adult or similar; content your provider doesn't mark and whose category name doesn't say so may still appear. If you give a kids profile an age band (0–6, 7–12, 13–15), content is also filtered by category, genre and channel name; this filtering happens on the device, relies on names and may not classify every item correctly. If you set a parental lock PIN, switching to a profile with a wider band or to a non-kids profile asks for it.

Changes

If a feature that sends data or stores new data is added, this page will be updated beforehand and the change recorded in the version history below.
