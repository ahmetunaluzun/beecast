Your IPTV subscription, one remote away.

Bee Cast plays Xtream, M3U and MAC portal playlists. You enter the details your provider gave you; live TV, movies and series come from your own list.

The app has no built-in channels or content, and sells none.

What's inside

Readable from across the room, usable with one hand.

Live TV and programme guide
Categories, what's on now and next, an hour-by-hour guide; catch-up if your provider supports it. If a stream drops, the app reconnects by itself.
Movies and series
Discover rows, continue where you left off, next episode, plot, director and cast. Mark what you have seen as "Watched".
Radio
Radio stations in your list get their own category in Live TV, and you can add your own station addresses. If the stream sends it, the song playing now is shown. On Android phones it keeps playing with the screen off, and you can pick stations from your car screen with Android Auto.
Profiles
Each profile keeps its own favourites and progress. Kids profiles have an age band.
Audio and subtitle language
Pick before or while watching; remembered per movie and series.
Search
Across channel, movie, series and programme names; movies also by original title, cast and director. Accents optional.
My devices
Move your list and settings from your phone to the TV over your home network; devices synced with your phone keep the same progress and favourites.
Text size
Normal, Large and Extra large. The layout holds up in all three.
Parental lock
Content your provider marks as adult stays hidden without a PIN.
Favourites and recently watched
Red key adds a favourite, yellow key jumps back to recent channels.
Six languages
Türkçe, English, Deutsch, Français, Español, العربية.
Five looks
One app, five screen layouts; switch any time in Settings.

About content

Bee Cast is a player, not a broadcaster.

The app contains, sells and recommends no channels, movies, series or playlists. Everything it plays comes from the list you add.

Only add content you have the right to watch. Your provider is responsible for what its list contains and for its legality.

Support

Something not working?

My playlist won't load.
Enter the server address, username and password exactly as your provider gave them. The app shows the reason on screen, for example when the provider can't be reached or the account has expired.
A channel won't open.
Your provider may not be broadcasting it right now. Try again in a few minutes; if other channels open, the problem is most likely with that stream.
How do I move my list to another TV or my phone?
Settings › My devices. Step-by-step guide with screenshots: Help.
Do you provide the channels?
No. Bee Cast provides no content; you need an IPTV subscription from a provider.

When you write, include your TV model, the app version (Settings › About) and what happened when you did what.
