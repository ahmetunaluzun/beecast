What Bee Cast doesn't have

  • Downloads (offline viewing)
  • Recording (DVR)
  • Multi-screen
  • Built-in channels, playlists or radio lists; the app contains no content
  • Ads and usage tracking (analytics)
  • A Bee Cast account or subscription
  • Uploading an M3U file (link only)
  • Movies and series from MAC portals
  • Changing channel by typing its number
  • Chromecast, picture-in-picture and swipe volume/brightness on iPhone/iPad
  • In-app update on Windows

Bee Cast is a player: everything it plays comes from the list you add.
