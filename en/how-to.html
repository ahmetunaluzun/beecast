How-to

Updated: 31 October 2026

The most-asked tasks in short narrated videos. The channels, movies and stations in the videos are made-up examples; your screen shows your own list. To move your lists and settings between devices: Help. What's included: Features.

Add a playlist

  1. On first start, choose your language and press OK.
  2. Pick a look (change it later in Settings › Appearance).
  3. Choose the type your provider gave you: M3U link or Xtream Codes (server, user name, password).
  4. Name the list and type the playlist link exactly as given.
  5. Press Connect; channels, movies and series arrive in a few seconds.
  6. More lists later: Settings › Playlists and account.

Live TV and the TV guide

  1. Press left and choose Live TV in the menu.
  2. Up/down arrows move through channels, left goes to categories.
  3. OK on a channel: preview. OK again: full screen.
  4. In full screen, up/down changes channel and Back returns to the list.
  5. Guide, to the right of the channel: left/right for time, up/down for channels; OK on what's on now opens that channel.

Pick up where you left off

  1. On the home screen, go to the movie in Continue watching and press OK.
  2. Resume plays from where you stopped, Start over goes back to the beginning.
  3. While playing, right/left skip 10 seconds; hold to go faster.
  4. Leave with Back and your place is saved; the same for every episode.

Profiles, kids profile and parental lock

  1. Settings › Profiles › Add profile.
  2. Give it a name and a colour.
  3. Tick Kids profile, choose the age group (0–6, 7–12, 13–15), Save.
  4. Settings › Parental lock › Turn on and set PIN: a 4-digit PIN, twice.
  5. The app asks "Who's watching?" at start; leaving the kids profile and opening Settings ask for the PIN.

Add an internet radio station

  1. Settings › Radio.
  2. Type the station name.
  3. Type the stream address from the station's own website (starts with http:// or https://). Finish typing and the station is added.
  4. Live TV › Radio category › OK on the station.

Which devices play radio?

On 31 October 2026 we tried 51 internet radio stations (MP3, AAC, AAC+, OGG and HLS; http and https) in the app itself:

DevicePlayedDidn't play
Android phone (emulator)49 / 51One station's server refused the stream (HTTP 401); one high-bitrate MP3 stream is being looked into.
Android TV (Google TV)49 / 51The same two streams.
Windows49 / 51Two MP3 streams on the same stream server; being looked into.
2016 Samsung TV36 / 51All of them https; these TVs now also try the http address (below).

On Android and Windows, stations in every format (MP3, AAC, AAC+, OGG, HLS) played; the few that didn't were station-specific, not format problems. We haven't run this test on newer Samsung TVs or iPhone/iPad yet.

Some stations don't open on an older Samsung TV.
2016 Samsung TVs can't open some https streams protected with newer certificates (seen in our test). On these TVs, if https fails Bee Cast tries the http version of the same address; 10 of the 19 stations we tried played that way. If the station's site lists an http address, you can add that one.
It says "The radio server did not provide the stream (HTTP …)".
The station's server isn't sending the stream: the station may be off air or its address may have changed. Add the current address from the station's website.
It says "This device could not connect to the radio server".
The device never reached the station. Check your internet connection and that the address is typed correctly.
No song title.
The station sends the song or programme title; if it doesn't, nothing is shown.
